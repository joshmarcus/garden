# Product specifications

These documents describe the architecture and design of context-garden as a whole.
They combine the product's design intent with lessons from implementation and
operation. Phase specifications provide focused detail for a body of work; these
product-level documents remain the architectural reference across phases.

- [System architecture and design](system-architecture.md): context, task graph,
  controller, execution, review, recovery, resource management and product learning.
- [Enterprise environment](enterprise-environment.md): integration boundaries,
  configuration, identity, managed workspaces and confidential evidence.
- [Persona reviews and retrospectives](persona-reviews.md): distinct narrative voices,
  structured findings and faithful retrospective synthesis.

- [True multiplayer gardens](multiplayer.md): proposed member identity, local schedulers,
  assigned work scopes, shared coordination, project focus and read-only publication.

Keep specifications coherent as the design evolves. Describe constraints, unresolved
design questions and gaps where they matter to understanding the system. Use generic
examples and exclude private environment details.
