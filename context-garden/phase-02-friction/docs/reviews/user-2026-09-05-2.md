The loop now runs, merges and explains itself: task and run pages show every run, cost, verdict and transcript, and 84 PRs landed in a day on the tool's own gates. But the surfaces that ask for my attention are not yet trustworthy: the Inbox counts 13 freeze-deferred drafts as decisions while filing the one real manual task under 'no action needed', a triage click on an already-merged task dispatched a paid review, the review-cap card fired twenty-plus times on rebase pushes, rebase rounds consumed the revision cap until I reset counters by hand, and the new retro command needed an undocumented flag to run correctly. Fix the attention surface and the rebase accounting and I would leave it running unwatched.

## High

- **inbox** — The Inbox reports '14 need you' when 13 are drafts deferred by the freeze and the one genuinely manual task (CG-029) is filed under 'Auto-retrying, no action needed' with only a Cancel button.
  - suggestion: Give drafts a park/defer state that leaves the decision count, and classify manual-runner ready tasks as a decision card with a 'take' action, not a retrying notice.
- **task actions** — Triaging a task that had already automerged moved CG-074 from done back to in_review and dispatched a $1.08 review of a merged PR.
  - suggestion: Refuse triage, review, retry and resume on done/cancelled/wont_do tasks in the scheduler, and drop stale cards on the next tick (CG-142).
- **review cap** — After the review cap, every push including pure rebases re-raises 'this PR is yours' and pings notify; the digest shows it 20+ times and each click spent a review round on an unchanged diff.
  - suggestion: Raise the review-cap card once per PR, skip re-review when the diff hash is unchanged after a rebase, and do not renotify on a card that is already up.
- **rebase cost** — Conflict rebases count against max_revisions, so CG-111 hit the cap twice on the same fixture conflict and needed two manual counter resets; four of its five revise rounds were rebases.
  - suggestion: Exempt rebase rounds from the revision cap and record them as their own mode so the Spend panel stops blaming the model tier (CG-139/CG-141).
